## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#1E3A8A) to convey trust and professionalism, drawing inspiration from the reliability and stability expected in financial systems and software solutions.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F4F8), providing a clean and modern backdrop that ensures readability and focuses attention on the content.
- Accent color: Teal (#2DD4BF) used strategically for calls to action and highlights, to draw attention and inject a sense of innovation and efficiency.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif, to offer a modern look with a touch of warmth and personality for comfortable reading.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif with high contrast, giving an elegant, fashionable feel that is suitable for headlines.
- Use clean and professional icons to represent services and features.
- Subtle animations for page transitions and content loading to enhance user experience.
